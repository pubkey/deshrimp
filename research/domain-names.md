# Domain names

The availability snapshot behind the name, kept so nobody has to re-run it.
**Checked 9 September 2026** against RDAP (`rdap.org`), which reports
registration status only. Availability moves — re-verify at a registrar before
buying anything below.

## Registered

**`deshrimp.com`** — registered 9 September 2026. The home of the page.

Why it, over the alternatives:

- **It is a verb.** *De-shrimp yourself.* It names what the page does to you
  rather than what you are. Every competitor's name describes the product
  instead — SitApp, Slouch Sniper, PostureGuard, SuperShrimp.
- **`de` is also German**, so the origin is in the name without shutting out the
  other eleven languages.
- **It is a brand, not a keyword**, which is the only kind of name still
  available (see the taken list). The keyword terms become paths —
  `/shrimp-posture`, `/desk-shrimp` — on a domain we own.
- **`.com` fits the trust story.** The pitch is "no server, nothing to hide,
  read the source"; a novelty TLD quietly undercuts that with exactly the
  audience most likely to refuse a camera prompt.
- It is already the repo name, so nothing has to be renamed.

## Why `de-` and not `un-`

Asked and settled, so it does not get re-litigated:

- **`de-` attaches to a noun and means remove the thing** — debone, defrost,
  detox, de-ice, decaffeinate. "Deshrimp" = take the shrimp out of you. That is
  exactly the claim.
- **`un-` reverses an action, not a state** — undo, untie, unfold, unlock. It
  needs a verb first. "Unshrimp" presupposes *to shrimp* as something you did,
  which is not how the meme works: you do not shrimp, you *are* one.
- **Where `un-` genuinely wins:** it is more internet-native (unfriend,
  uninstall, unsubscribe) and reads a shade lighter. `de-` carries a faint
  clinical-technical scent — debug, defrost. That is a real point against, but
  for this page the technical scent is an asset: it sits well beside the
  honest-engineering framing in
  [`what-a-front-webcam-can-measure.md`](./what-a-front-webcam-can-measure.md).
- Said aloud, "deshrimp" is clean and "unshrimp" mushes on the n-ʃ cluster.
